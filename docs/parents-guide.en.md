## 1. 💡 The basic idea

FamilyMission turns household chores into small missions with **instant feedback**: the child ticks something off, hears a jingle and watches their ring grow. That immediacy is what carries children through the day.

- **Own progress instead of competition:** every child has their own monthly goal. The tiles stay in a fixed order, and there is no ranking among siblings.
- **Family goal:** together you fill the family rocket. Everyone pulls in the same direction.
- **Collect first, then redeem:** points go into a balance that is exchanged for rewards in the shop. This teaches saving and looking forward to something.

The effect depends mostly on you as parents: **consistent** (the rules apply every day), **friendly** (tone instead of pressure) and **steady** (not strict today and generous tomorrow). The app is the tool, you are the frame.

> 💡 Rule of thumb: better a few rules that reliably apply than many that nobody keeps up.

## 2. 🛠️ Setup in 10 minutes

- **Kid profiles + PIN:** settings card, Family tab, Kids section: enable the person as a kid, pick an avatar and colour, enter the birth year (or birthday) and a 4-digit secret number. The PIN stops siblings from ticking things off for each other.
- **Parent role:** in the same tab (Parents section) you decide who may approve, praise and give points. That keeps the wall tablet harmless for kids.
- **Quick start:** the 🚀 Kids mode quick start button proposes age-appropriate tasks, points and goals and creates everything with one click. Six steps (the “We parents” step is optional), also on a phone.
- **Running the quick start again:** with “Reconcile earlier wizard tasks” the wizard **never resets what you customized**. As soon as you change something on a wizard task (name, points, steps, times, repeat …), FamilyMission remembers it; those changes survive every further run, everything else is updated. A customized task you deselect is not deleted but becomes one of your own tasks; uncustomized, deselected tasks are removed. The summary shows beforehand “N customized tasks stay unchanged · M will be updated · K will be removed · J will become your own tasks” and lets you deliberately “reset to proposal” single tasks. With **Undo the last wizard run** (first step of the wizard, for 7 days) you restore the changed tasks.
- **Tablet dashboard (kiosk):** the kids card in full screen on a wall tablet (for example with Fully Kiosk Browser, landscape). Kids tap their tile, enter the PIN and see their missions.
- **Own devices (`mode: mine`):** with `mode: mine` the card shows the tasks of the logged-in person right away, without tiles and PIN. Ideal for the phone or tablet of an older child.
- **Bonus triggers:** a bonus can open by itself, for example when the dishwasher has finished (settings card, Tasks & points tab, Bonus triggers section). The tablet can announce it by voice.
- **Waste collection:** with the HACS integration “Waste Collection Schedule” the bonus task “Put the bins out” opens the evening before a collection (empty the bins in the flat, bins out, check there is room), and “Bring the bins in” on the evening of the collection day. You decide per bin whether the kids may do it (e.g. glass stays with you), from which age (default 6) and whether you approve before the points are credited (settings card, Tasks & points tab, Waste collection section).
- **Voice announcement:** new bonus tasks can be announced on speakers, e.g. “Dad created a bonus task: carry up the shopping. 3 points.” Selectable for spontaneous bonuses, waste tasks, bonus triggers and reached savings goals (“Anna reached the savings goal: cinema.”); silent at night (settings card, Notifications tab, Voice announcement section, with a test button).
- **Reminders:** a time can be set per task. If the task is still open then, an event or notification is sent.

> 💡 Plan 10 minutes for the setup, and afterwards take a quiet moment to explain the system to the kids.

## 3. 🌱 The first weeks (ramp-up)

A good start decides a lot. Increase slowly: the system should make kids want to take part, not overwhelm them.

- **Week 1–2:** only a few missions (morning, evening, one table duty), **no deductions**, lots of praise. The shop holds small rewards that can be reached within a few days. This way kids experience early on that it pays off.
- **Week 3–4:** normal phase with all planned tasks. You check spot samples instead of every task (the sample rate can be set per kid).
- **After that:** new elements (a new task, a new reward, a new goal) at most every 2–3 weeks. Too much new at once destroys the routine.

Kids who need a lot of structure benefit most from fixed times, few steps per mission and very predictable routines. Start smaller with them and stay in the first phase longer.

> 💡 The quick start knows the phases “Start” (goal: 60 % of the maximum) and “Normal” (75 %). The first, partial month is calculated pro rata.

## 4. 📅 The daily routine

**What the kids see:**

- **Time windows:** tasks are only active at the right time. Before that, a symbol shows when it starts.
- **Weekend and “Later today”:** a task can have its own window for Saturday / Sunday (e.g. morning mission 06:00–11:00 at the weekend; the task editor). On the tablet, tasks that only start later in the day are not shown as a locked card but in one line “🕑 Später heute: Hausaufgaben ab 13:30 · Zimmer-Blitz ab 16:00”; 30 minutes before the start the card appears with a countdown.
- **Homework and “only after”:** with the household feature “school” the quick start proposes “Homework / study time” (from age 6, Monday to Friday only, 13:30–18:00, 3 points, steps like clear the desk, homework done, tick off the book). The room blitz only opens once the kid’s homework is done or none is due today (weekend, school holidays, absence); until then it appears in the line as “Zimmer-Blitz nach Hausaufgaben”. Per task you can switch this on and off with “Only after homework”.
- **Weekdays:** instead of “every N days” a task can be due on fixed weekdays (for example Mon, Wed, Fri). If it is not done, that day lapses without points and without a deduction, and it continues on the next listed weekday.
- **Taking turns:** if you pick two or more kids and “Taking turns” for a task, every occurrence belongs to exactly one kid; only that kid sees the task (“It's your turn today”). After it is done or missed, the next kid is up.
- **Steps:** small steps to tick (get dressed, make the bed). “Done!” only works once all steps are ticked. The quick start adds fitting steps to almost every task (filtered by age and household, e.g. brushing teeth and the lunch box in the missions); on a re-run only tasks you did not customize get the new steps.
- **Medication:** if you added a tablet, drops or an inhaler to a child (settings → Family tab → kid → “💊 Medication”), it shows up as one more step in the morning or evening mission with “✓ Taken” and “Not today”. Both answers count; only no answer holds “Done!” back. It never gives points and nobody is judged. You choose where the step sits in the medication settings (“Position: after step …”, also in the “New task → Medication” sheet); by default it follows “Have breakfast” in the morning mission and otherwise comes last.
- **Done:** gives points, confetti and a sound right away, or waits for your approval.
- **👫 Together:** where allowed, several kids do a task together. The points are shared, plus a team bonus.
- **Bonus:** voluntary extra tasks. Whoever taps “Take it” reserves it for a few minutes.
- **Shop:** exchange the balance for rewards and choose a savings goal.

**What you do:**

- **Approvals:** in the parents card you confirm what needs approval (or directly from a push notification on your phone).
- **Praise:** with ❤️ Praise you send a sticker and a few words.
- **Spontaneous bonus:** a one-off extra job (“fetch bread”) in a few taps.
- **Skip today:** a task drops out today (sick, outing), with no points and no penalty.
- **Postpone:** move the task to a later date.
- **Complete for the kid:** you record a task that was done, for example when the tablet was not at hand.
- **Swap a turn:** on the Kids tab, “Taking turns” shows “Today: Lena · next time: Max” per task. “Instead of Lena: Max” lets another kid take today's occurrence (Lena is next afterwards); “Skip turn” passes it on to the next kid without making it up.
- **Health today:** on the Today tab you see per child whether the medication was taken; “✓ given” records that you gave it yourself. You get a push when a child chose “Not today” and pressed “Done” in the mission (until then the answer can still change; at the deadline at the latest) or does not answer by the deadline (at night it waits until morning). An optional alert tells you when “Not today” happens too often. The Kids tab shows the last 7 days.
- **Stock and refill:** a medication can optionally track a pill stock (Settings → Family → kid → “💊 Medication” or in the “New task → Medication” sheet, plus “Pills per dose”). Every “taken” counts one dose down, a changed answer gives it back. When the stock lasts only seven more days (depending on the medication’s weekdays) FamilyMission creates the parent task “💊 Prescription / refill …” once. Entering a higher stock removes it. Empty = off. The running stock is kept apart from the entered value: saving the settings with an outdated form keeps the counted-down stock; only a changed number restarts it.
- **Undo:** ticked by mistake? The entry can be taken back and the points are deducted again.

<a id="approval"></a>

## 5. ✅ Approval, “Not done” and “Cheated”

Some tasks need your approval (set per task, plus optional spot checks per kid). A completion only counts once you confirm.

- **Approve:** everything is fine, the points go to the account.
- **Not done:** well meant but not quite finished, or forgotten. The task is open again, **without a deduction**.
- **Cheated:** only for **deliberate** cheating (ticked but not done). There is a deduction equal to the task points (adjustable) and a strict phase.

**Important:** a deduction only for deliberate cheating, **never for forgetting**. Deductions are **private**: they only appear in the kid's own view, never on the home screen or in front of siblings. The **strict phase** (default: 7 days, every task needs approval) **ends automatically**.

**Approval push:** when a task waits for approval, all adults get a push message (“✅ Clara is done”, “Clara & David are done” for a task done together) with the buttons **Approve** and **Not done**, straight from the lock screen. “Cheated” is deliberately only in the app. Once handled (wherever), a second tap does nothing. Can be switched off with `approval_push` (settings, “Notifications” tab). Handled pushes disappear from the other devices (silent `clear_notification` command, visible in the Companion app history); switch off “Remove handled pushes from the other devices” if you do not want that.

**Approvals do not sit unseen:** in the settings (“Notifications” tab) every adult shows where the push goes (✓ automatically via the Companion app or a chosen target, ⚠ no target) with a **Test push** button. If nobody can be reached, the parents' hub shows a yellow hint and Home Assistant a notification. If an approval waits longer than `approval_reminder_hours` (default 2 hours, 0 = off), there is **one** quiet reminder (“⏳ Approval still waiting”), at night only after waking up. Once the approval is handled, the push disappears for **all** parents.

> 💡 Talk instead of punishing: briefly and calmly discuss what happened and how it can go better. The deduction is only the occasion for the conversation.

<a id="rewards"></a>

## 6. 🎁 Choosing rewards well

- **Experiences and privileges first:** a trip together, choosing game night, staying up 30 minutes later. Things and screen time come after that, sparingly.
- **Price levels:** small (10–30 points, reachable within a few days), medium (50–100, in 1–2 weeks), large (150 and more, by the end of the month).
- **Savings goal reachable in 1–2 weeks:** what is too far away does not motivate. Pick the savings goal so that normal effort gets there in one to two weeks.
- **Choose together:** ask the kids what they really want and rotate the offer regularly. Otherwise it gets boring.

> 💡 Screen time as a reward turns it into the yardstick for everything else. If you offer it, keep it small and limited.

<a id="praise"></a>

## 7. ❤️ Praising

- **Specific:** “You set the table all by yourself, even with napkins!” instead of “Well done”.
- **Promptly:** praise works within minutes. The next day it hardly does.
- **Honestly:** praise what really worked. Effort and sticking with it count more than the result.

The **❤️ Praise** button complements the personal word, it does not replace it. Kids see the sticker big the next time they open their view, without points and without comparison. When you are home, say it in person, too.

> 💡 Plenty works without a button: a quick look, a thumbs up, a hug.

## 8. 🚀 Monthly goals, family rocket, ring above 100 %

- **Monthly goal:** every child has their own points goal. The ring around the avatar shows progress.
- **Ring above 100 %:** whoever does more spins further laps in darker colours. More is allowed, but never necessary.
- **Family rocket:** all points together fill the rocket up to the family goal (for example a pizza night). Only confirmed task points count, no bonuses and no deductions.
- **Goal in percent:** instead of fixed points you can choose “Percent of possible points” in the settings (default 75 %). At the start of the month the maximum the kids can earn with their duties is calculated once; the family goal is the chosen percentage of it, rounded to 5. During the month the goal can only go **down** (task removed or paused, day skipped, birthday, lower percent), never up: new tasks and a higher percent apply from next month. The settings show, for example, “November: 410 points (75 % of 547, as of 1.11.) · from 1.12. probably 460”. Bonus tasks count towards progress but not towards the possible points; medication never counts.
- **Absences:** More → “Abwesenheit”: 🌴 family on a trip, 🎒 kid away (grandma, class trip), 🤒 sick or 📚 school holidays, with from / to dates. Chores and reminders pause and nothing counts as missed; after the last day the tasks come back and the family goal is lowered automatically. On a trip personal tasks (missions, routines) pause too unless you tick “keep personal tasks”; medication keeps going (for a kid who is away only with the option). The tablet shows “Urlaub bis …” or “Get well soon”; “End today” stops an absence early. Optional: a push “Seid ihr verreist?” when everybody has been away for a day (settings → We parents). School holidays only pause school things (homework, the school bag and lunch box steps; medication and everything else keep running). If you like, pick a calendar in the settings (Family): all-day events in it count as school holidays automatically.
- **New task:** Today or Kids tab → “➕ New task” (admins / managers only). Step 1: search or pick a template (frequent suggestions, your own templates, “No template”; Enter creates a task with the typed name). Step 2: for whom (with several kids “Each kid” = one task per kid, “Taking turns” = one task with rotation, “Together / open” = a bonus for everyone), below that the shared task editor (the same as in the task and template dialogs): icon and name on top with a live summary, kind (duty / bonus / errand), when (daily, weekdays, every N days, once on a date, time window, “Different on the weekend”), steps (reorder by dragging or with the arrows), reward (points, approval, together) and rules (minimum age, home / personal, reminder, note). “💊 Medication” opens a small dialog instead and adds the medication for the chosen kids.
- **Month change:** at the start of the month the monthly points are reset and the month is archived. The **shop balance is kept**.

> 💡 Set the goal so that a child with normal effort reaches about 75 % of the maximum. That keeps it reachable without every day having to be perfect.

## 9. 👪 We parents: parent tasks and the parents hub

The adults' housework is work too, and often invisible. **We parents** makes it visible and shares it fairly: no points, no ranking, no blame. Set it up once, then just tick it off.

- **Parents hub:** the parents card has three tabs: **Today** (approvals and your parent tasks), **Kids** and **More** (settings, Sunday summary, history).
- **Setting up:** the quick start has an optional **We parents** step. Pick from a list of templates such as dinner, meal plan, shopping, bathrooms, finances or check-ups. A small selection is already ticked.
- **Me / You / Take turns:** for each task you decide who is responsible. With “Take turns” the task passes to the other person after every completion, without anyone keeping score.
- **No points:** parent tasks have no points, no balance and no goals. All that counts is done or not done.
- **Quiet carry-over:** whatever is left today quietly moves to tomorrow. No red, no “overdue”, no reminder, no blame. This applies to recurring tasks and to tasks without a date. Only when you set a date yourself does the task stay under “Overdue” until it is done.
- **Task list:** the **＋** next to “My tasks” adds a task in seconds: type the name, pick Today / Tomorrow / Weekend / Date or “Someday”, press Enter. The list shows **Overdue**, **Today**, **Coming up** and **Someday** (no date); tick with ✓. “**→ Tomorrow**” moves a task to tomorrow with one tap. “More options” holds time, repeat, duration and the planning flag.
- **Morning overview and time:** the settings (Notifications tab) can switch on a morning overview (“Today: 3 tasks, 1 overdue”, once a day per adult, only when something is due, off by default). A task with a time reminds the responsible adult once at that time. Nothing is sent at night (night mode).
- **More parent tasks:** tasks such as bathrooms, meal plan, calendar check, finances, fridge, vacuuming, kids’ clothes, check-ups or holiday childcare come with a **checklist** (bathrooms: toilet, sink & mirror, shower/bath, floor, towels). The steps only show progress (“2/5”), ✓ always works; after completing, the list starts afresh. Add your own steps under “More options” of the ＋ (one per line) or later in the editor. Also there: **weekdays** (e.g. Mon/Wed/Fri) instead of a fixed rhythm.
- **Shopping list:** the tasks shopping list, grocery shopping and fridge can be linked to a Home Assistant shopping list (`todo`) (More → Parent tasks → “Days, steps, shopping list”). The card then shows how many items are open; “Fridge / supplies” gets “🛒 To the shopping list”. Without a linked list nothing changes.
- **New templates:** **one-on-one time** (one task per kid, “One-on-one time with Lena”, alternating, weekly, 30 min, preselected in the quick start), family meeting / weekly review (Sundays), plan some time for myself, check the medicine cabinet, heating / filters / seasonal check and vet / vaccination (only with pets). Your changes to a template task (name, duration, steps, weekdays) are kept when you run the setup again.
- **Automatic:** with the waste collection the **bins** task is only due on the evening before a collection; if the kids did their “put the bins out” bonus the parent task closes silently, otherwise you get “The bins are not out yet” at the set time (19:30). When a kid strips the **bed linen**, “Change the bed linen” is due the same day (“Put on fresh bedding / wash”). Optionally (Settings → Notifications, off by default) FamilyMission creates a task “🔋 Battery …” for every **battery sensor** below the limit (20 %), once per sensor, checked every 30 minutes. No push at night. Home and car batteries and SoC / limit values (e.g. “Generator SOC Limit”) are skipped automatically; exclude other sensors with the button “Not a battery to replace” on the task or in the settings (“Excluded sensors”).
- **🧠 Planning:** meal plan, shopping list or calendar check are thinking work (mental load). Only these get a gentle reminder the evening before (“Tomorrow: meal plan, about 20 min.”). Set the time in the settings, Notifications tab.
- **Can you?:** if today does not work, ask your partner with one tap. They receive a message with “I'll do it” or “Can't”. If they take it, that applies to this one time only.
- **Sunday summary:** on Sunday evening a neutral note arrives: how many hours each person did this week and what was left over. It judges nothing, it only makes talking easier. It can be switched off in the settings.
- **Role-model band:** the kids tablet shows a quiet line such as “Mom and Dad also have 3 missions today”, with progress dots and no points. Kids see that everybody pitches in. The band and its wording are configurable.
- **Own device:** with `mode: mine` your own parent tasks (no points, with duration) appear on your own phone and can be ticked off there. The round **＋** in the header of “My tasks” adds a quick to-do right there (name, Today / Tomorrow / Weekend / Date / Someday, optional time and, with a partner, “For” me/partner); undated and upcoming tasks sit in the folds “Someday” and “Coming up”, “→ Tomorrow” postpones.

> 💡 Start small: five to eight tasks are enough. Leave out whatever does not fit. There are no required fields.

## 10. 🌙 Rocket launch, night mode and redeem push

Three features for the wall tablet that give the day and the reward a clear frame: the family goal is **celebrated together**, the day **comes to an end**, and the kids' wishes **reach you right away**.

- **Rocket launch:** when the family reaches its goal, the tablet shows “All aboard the rocket!” with the reward and a countdown (default 60 seconds, adjustable from 15 to 180). Everybody gathers, everybody sees it, then the rocket lifts off. It is deliberately a shared moment, not a silent points account.
- **Once per goal:** the launch happens once per monthly goal. Even if points are taken back later, a reached goal stays reached. You get a push message (“Family goal reached! Reward: … plan it together now”) so the reward does not get lost in everyday life.
- **Sequence:** a call sound rings every 10 seconds, the last 10 seconds count down with big numbers, and “Launch now!” shortens it. Then come the liftoff, fireworks and a finishing picture. Several tablets finish cleanly, and the family bar then reads “🚀 launched!”. If a device prefers reduced motion, a calmer variant is shown.
- **Speaker:** at the start the event `family_mission_rocket_countdown` fires. A speaker can announce the countdown with it (example in `examples/kids/automationen.yaml`).
- **Night mode:** at bedtime (default 20:30) the tablet shows a dark sleep screen: a sleeping little moon buddy with a nightcap and an analogue alarm clock whose coloured hand and arc show the wake-up time and the remaining sleep (“Good night”, later “Shh … everyone is asleep”, shortly before waking “Morning is almost here!”, plus “I'm still sleeping for 7 h 30 min”), without tasks, sounds or bright areas. The day is clearly over, and there is no pressure to catch up in the evening. A tap makes the screen a little brighter and the alarm clock wobbles for 5 seconds. Parents (admin, manager, parent role) keep the normal view on their own devices.
- **Times:** it sleeps until the wake-up time of the following day: Mon to Fri 06:00, Sat and Sun 08:00 (so the night to Saturday ends at 08:00, the night to Monday at 06:00). A kid session still open at bedtime runs out normally and is never cut off mid-input. In the morning a “Good morning ☀️” banner greets everyone for 30 minutes.
- **Quiet nights:** no reminders for kid tasks are sent during the night. If the family goal was reached at night, the rocket launches after waking up. Everything can be adjusted or switched off in the **Tablet & devices** tab of the settings; an own device (`mode: mine`) is never affected.
- **Redeem push:** when a kid wants to redeem something in the shop, all adults get a push message (“🎁 Clara wants to redeem”) with the buttons **Redeem** and **Decline**, straight from the lock screen. Once it is handled (wherever), no further message arrives. Can be switched off with `reward_push`.

> 💡 Plan the reward the same evening: a cinema night that is actually in the calendar works far better than “sometime”.

## 11. 🌿 Phasing out

The aim is **habit, not collecting points**. When a routine has run stably for 4 weeks, lead it step by step away from the reward:

- First fewer points for the task (for example from 3 to 2).
- Then remove the steps of the mission. The child knows them by now.
- Finally let the task continue without points, or take it out of the app altogether.

Pass the attention that frees up to a new habit, but only one at a time (see ramp-up).

> 💡 Tell the kids beforehand: “Brushing teeth works great. From next week there are no points for it anymore, you can do that already!”

## 12. ❓ Frequent questions / problems

- **My child ticks everything off without doing it.** Turn on approval or spot checks for those tasks and take a quick look. Talk about it honestly. Use “Cheated” only for deliberate cheating, calmly and with a conversation. Visible steps (for example “bed made”) make checking easier.
- **Arguments about the bonus.** Whoever taps “Take it” has priority for a few minutes. You can allow “Together” for tasks, give a spontaneous bonus to one specific kid, or set a minimum age.
- **Kid does not want points any more.** That is normal after a few weeks. Renew the rewards, focus more on experiences, phase out routines that run well and ask what the child is missing. Points are never a means of pressure.
- **Illness / holidays.** Pause tasks in the task editor (pause until a date) or use “Skip today” for single days. On the birthday, duties are automatically off. For long breaks, adjust the monthly goal.
- **Siblings of different ages.** Tasks have a minimum age, every child has their own goal. Keep the effort per minute comparable, not the number of tasks. Older kids can help younger ones with “Together”.
- **Tablet shows an old version.** That is the browser cache. Reload the page (in Fully Kiosk Browser: clear the cache and reload), in the companion app clear the frontend cache. After a FamilyMission update, the new version appears after a Home Assistant restart and a reload.
- **A new bonus does not show up?** Tap the logo: the small rocket icon next to the title (kids tablet: home screen, or the header with “My tasks”; parents' hub: header) reloads everything at once and confirms with “Updated ✓”. Normally this happens by itself: after a lost connection, when the tablet wakes up and every 5 minutes. If it does not help, check the connection to Home Assistant (“No connection”) and reload the page.
